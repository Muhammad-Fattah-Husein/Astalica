## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modest luxury with serene poise, fluid grace, and couture refinement. Tailored for high-end modest fashion, it bridges traditional dignity with contemporary softness, softening rigid architectural lines into flowing, ergonomic contours.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on:
- **Curved Editorial Minimalism**: Generous ivory whitespace, airy compositions, and continuous, rounded contours across every touchpoint.
- **Organic Warmth & Tactility**: Rich pearlescent cream canvases, lustrous brushed gold accents, and deep forest emerald accents that offer prestige without harshness.
- **Poetic Typography**: Expressive editorial serif typography paired harmoniously with clean, modern sans-serifs.

The experience evokes calmness, bespoke care, exclusivity, and understated elegance, inviting users into a seamless digital atelier.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from silk weaves, natural dyes, and golden illumination:

- **Primary (`#C5A059`) — Warm Satin Gold**: Reserved for luminous focal points, delicate badges, active tabs, and premium framing.
- **Secondary (`#1B3B2B`) — Deep Emerald Green**: Serves as the anchor of authority, modest dignity, and highest visual priority. Applied to primary call-to-actions, prominent badges, and brand iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#E8DEC8`) — Champagne Sand**: Provides whisper-soft structure for pill borders, dividers, and subtle outlines without visual tension.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1A`) — Soft Charcoal Noir**: Provides high-contrast, fatigue-free readability for editorial titles and body typography against cream surfaces.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FDFBF7`) — Pearl Ivory**: The foundational viewport base, complemented by `#F7F3EA` for inset containers and secondary pill surfaces.

### Functional Gradients & Tints
- **Gold Luster Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #DFC27D 0%, #C5A059 50%, #A8833E 100%)` for luxury seals, selected indicators, and limited-edition badges.
- **Emerald Glow**: `rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.08)` for tactile button focus rings and state transitions.

## Typography

The typography system pairs the editorial elegance of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, modern geometric flow of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: `Playfair Display` drives collection storytelling, lookbook banners, and bespoke titles. Tracking is tightened slightly on prominent display scales to emulate printed fashion periodicals.
- **Body & Functional UI**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides crisp readability across multi-line garment descriptions, dimensions, checkout forms, and pill-shaped interactive controls.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-labels**: Handled by `label-md` and `label-sm` with uppercase transformation and extended tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to lend a spacious, curated cadence to tags and chip labels.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout preserves balanced editorial proportions across devices:

- **Desktop (≥1024px)**: 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, centered with a max-width container of `1360px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

Component padding hierarchy:
- Pill elements, inputs, and search fields use horizontal breathing space of `space-lg` to accommodate full-radius curves naturally.
- Surface cards and modal dialogs maintain generous interior padding between `space-lg` and `space-xl`.
- Storytelling sections utilize `space-xl` * 2 of empty vertical separation to prevent clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient shadows harmonized with delicate champagne borders:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Pearl Ivory `#FDFBF7` with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Pill Chips, Floating Badges, Cards)**: White `#FFFFFF` or Ivory Tint `#F7F3EA`, framed with `1px solid #E8DEC8`, accompanied by soft tinted shadow: `0px 6px 24px -4px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Pill Menus, Floating Bars)**: `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #E8DEC8`, with elevated soft shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Lookbook Overlays)**: Full viewport backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.4)` tint, crowned with a layered shadow `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly committed to full roundedness (`ROUND_FULL` / `rounded-full` / `9999px`):

- **Interactive Elements**: Every button, input field, chip, tag, pill badge, fabric selector, and pagination bubble must employ a full pill radius (`border-radius: 9999px`). There are zero sharp or rectangular corners on interactive targets.
- **Structural Containers & Large Surfaces**: Modals, slide-overs, and editorial card surfaces adopt ultra-soft radii (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl` / `24px` to `32px`), complementing the pill geometry while maintaining visual structure.
- **Media Tiles**: Editorial photography and garment lookbook viewports feature softened `24px` corners, maintaining harmony with the organic, rounded language.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Conversion & Purchase)**: Rendered in Deep Emerald Green (`#1B3B2B`) with Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) text. Border radius is strictly `rounded-full` (`9999px`). Padding is `0.875rem 2rem` (`space-sm` vertical, `space-xl` horizontal). Hover transitions smoothly to `#2C533E` with an ambient glow.
- **Secondary (Bespoke Exploration)**: Transparent background, `1.5px solid #C5A059`, Warm Satin Gold typography (`#C5A059`), with `border-radius: 9999px`. On hover, fills with `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost Button**: Charcoal Noir (`#1A1A1A`) text, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover, gains a subtle tint of `#F7F3EA`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Text Inputs & Search Controls**: Encased in a continuous pill envelope (`border-radius: 9999px`). Surface is Pearl Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) or White, bordered by `1.5px solid #E8DEC8`. Padding is `0.75rem 1.5rem` to keep text securely inset from the rounded edges.
- **Active Focus**: Border elevates to `#C5A059` with a subtle focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#7F7667`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Category & Fabric Filters (Chiffon, Silk, Voile)**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`), `1px solid #E8DEC8`, background `#FDFBF7`, padding `0.5rem 1.25rem`. 
- **Selected Filter State**: Deep Emerald Green (`#1B3B2B`) background with Pearl Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) text, or Warm Satin Gold (`#C5A059`) with soft contrasting text.
- **Luxury Badges**: Metallic gradient fill (`#DFC27D` to `#A8833E`) with Deep Emerald text, completely pill-shaped, rendered in `label-sm` with tracking `0.08em`.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Navigation Links & Segmented Controls**: Grouped inside a container pill (`#F7F3EA`, `border-radius: 9999px`, padding `4px`), where the active tab is an elevated white or emerald pill (`border-radius: 9999px`) that glides across states.
- **Editorial Lists**: Separated by ultra-light `#E8DEC8` dividers with generous vertical padding (`space-md`), featuring rounded list indicators.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radios**: Fully circular outer ring (`9999px`) in `1.5px solid #E8DEC8`, filling with Deep Emerald (`#1B3B2B`) and a Warm Satin Gold central dot on selection.
- **Checkboxes**: Smoothly rounded squares (`border-radius: 8px`), adopting `#1B3B2B` fill upon check with an Ivory check icon.

### Cards & Merchandising Surfaces
- **Product Tiles**: Crisp ivory card with `24px` rounded corners and `1px solid #E8DEC8`. 
- **Image Container**: Top corners follow the `24px` radius, showcasing garments in a soft 4:5 vertical proportion.
- **Floating Quick-Add / Swatches**: Color swatches are circular (`9999px`) with dual concentric rings; floating action overlays use pill buttons with `9999px` radius.
